How to give feedback

  1. Add a few of your own stocks with ➕ Add your own stocks (on Overview, Holdings or Portfolio setup).
  2. Click the tabs and try things: open a stock from Holdings or Today's movers, change chart periods, filter alerts.
  3. Rate each screen with the stars underneath it.
  4. To point at something specific, mention the screen and what you clicked in the boxes here.
  5. Fill in the questions and press the button under them, then send the copied text to the person who shared this page (WhatsApp or email is fine).

Nothing you type or add on this page is sent anywhere automatically. The stocks you add stay in your browser only.
